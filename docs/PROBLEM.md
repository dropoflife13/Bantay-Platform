# Problem Statement: Bantay LGU Platform

## The Problem
Local government units (LGUs) in the Philippines struggle to formalize and track local micro-economies (wet markets, ambulant vendors, street food stalls). Stalls exchange cash continuously, but municipal records rely on manual paper ledgers or fragmented spreadsheets.

Key operational pain points:
1. **Connectivity Deserts:** Concrete public markets and open street vendor spots have spotty 3G/4G connectivity, causing standard web apps to fail or lose transactions.
2. **Revenue Leakage & Unverified Zones:** Ambiguous boundaries lead to unregistered vendors operating outside sanctioned zones or within restricted pedestrian corridors.
3. **Vendor Distrust over Financial Records:** Vendors fear micro-financing records or informal customer credits (*Utang*) will be seized or over-audited by tax assessors.
4. **Audit Immutability & Duplicate Submissions:** Retrying failed network requests often leads to double-counted sales or conflicting daily totals.

## The Person
- **Primary User:** Elena, a wet market fish stallholder who frequently loses internet signal during morning rush hours and needs a fast, simple point-of-sale that never loses a receipt.
- **Secondary User:** Arthur, a Market Administrator who needs to ensure vendors operate within designated municipal boundaries and track accurate market occupancy and daily volume.
